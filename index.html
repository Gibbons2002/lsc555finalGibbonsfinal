Search tools are a major part of library systems across the country and the world as they help patrons find what books they are looking for. Meta search engines are used to search multiple entries at once, they are expected to return more comprehensive and relevant results in a more efficient way. In terms of library search engines, they are considered facets. Facets are attributes of an object, categories, mutually exclusive and contain controlled vocabulary elements.

In terms of the pros and cons listed for both faceted and meta search engines, they cover lots of ground while also being based on a rank strategy. The pros for facet search engines are that individual search engines don’t cover all the web by themselves, less effort and provides a bigger picture. While on the other end, the cons are ranking strategy and combining data in multiple formats. Together, these bring out how faceted search engines are the main reason why libraries use them.
